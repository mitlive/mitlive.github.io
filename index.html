About mitlive

mitlive is a small group of builders and tinkerers. The work spans networking, connected hardware, healthcare access, accessibility, rural connectivity and public safety.

Most of what's here started because someone had a problem worth solving. A campus running on ageing infrastructure. A consulate buried in paperwork. A village with no usable bandwidth. Someone who needs to be heard without typing a sentence first.

The catalog stays current as things ship. Some entries link to live builds, some to write-ups, and a few stay quiet.
